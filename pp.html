1 Summary (Plain Language)

Floorly is an offline-first app. The home, room, photo, voice-note, checklist, and floor-plan data you create is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to our servers.

However, Floorly is not a "nothing ever leaves your device" app. To run ads, understand crashes, measure usage, and process subscriptions, the app integrates third-party SDKs that transmit specific technical data off the device:

  • Advertising SDKs (Google AdMob, AppLovin MAX) collect your advertising identifier and device/ad-interaction data to serve ads, including personalized ads, subject to your explicit consent.
  • Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, and Performance Monitoring send usage events, crash/diagnostic data, and performance traces to Google.
  • RevenueCat and the App Store / Google Play process your purchase and subscription state.
  • If you are a Pro user and enable Cloud Backup, your backup file is uploaded directly to your own Google Drive (Android) or iCloud (iOS) — not to us.

3 Data Stored Only on Your Device

The following data is stored locally within the app's secure SQLite/Drift database and private media folder. We do not have access to this data:

  • Property records: name, address, price, deal type, room configuration, area, floor, building age, dues, notes, star rating, visit date.
  • Location coordinates — only if you explicitly trigger the location capture feature.
  • Facade orientation — captured locally from the device compass.
  • Rooms: type, dimensions, doors/windows, tags, notes.
  • Photos (stored locally as binary files).
  • Voice notes (Pro feature).
  • Checklists, scoring weights, and 2D floor-plan layouts.

This data remains on your device unless you actively choose to export it or enable Cloud Backup. Uninstalling the App deletes this local data permanently.

7 Cloud Backup & Google API Limited Use

If you upgrade to Pro and enable Cloud Backup, a local backup archive is transmitted directly to your own personal cloud account.

  • Android (Google Drive): Backup data is confined to a hidden, isolated private app data folder via the drive.appdata scope — inaccessible to other apps and invisible in your standard Drive view.
  • iOS (iCloud): Backup data is saved to your personal iCloud container tied to your Apple ID.

Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance (Limited Use): Floorly's use of Google API data adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for serving ads, building user profiles, or transmitting to third parties.

8 Sharing and Export

When executing a PDF/PNG export or manual backup export, the application generates the file locally and passes it to the native system share sheet. The file is transmitted exclusively to the destination you select. This transfer occurs outside our software layer.

9 Advertising Identifiers and Opt-Out Mechanisms

Free-tier users who have confirmed they are 18 or older may be shown advertisements. Subject to your consent, ad networks may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA/AAID) to measure interactions and personalize ads. If you decline consent, only non-personalized (contextual) ads are shown.

  • Consent Frameworks: We implement Google UMP complying with the IAB Europe TCF.
  • OS-Level Control:
    • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking
    • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete/Reset Advertising ID
  • Pro Tier: Any Pro subscription completely deactivates all advertising SDK initialization.

10 Data Retention

  • On-Device Data: Retained indefinitely until you clear app data, delete records, or uninstall Floorly.
  • Cloud Backups: Managed by you; retained until deleted by you.
  • Telemetry & Crash Analytics: Retained by Google Firebase for up to 14 months before automated deletion.
  • Financial Receipts: Logged by RevenueCat and app stores in accordance with financial retention laws.

13 Children's Privacy

Floorly is designed for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18.

If a user indicates they are under 18: no advertising SDKs are initialized, no advertising identifiers are accessed, Firebase Analytics is disabled, and access to the App is restricted.

If you are a parent/guardian and believe a minor provided data through Floorly, contact insep9597@gmail.com.

14 Data Security

Local files are isolated in the OS application sandbox with hardware encryption, biometric access, and passcode protection. The SQLite/Drift layer uses parameterized queries to eliminate SQL injection risks. All network traffic from third-party SDKs enforces TLS/HTTPS protocols.

17 Data Erasure & In-App Data Deletion

You can permanently erase all local data at any time via Settings → Delete My Data. This triggers a complete purge that:

  1. Permanently destroys the local database (SQLite/Drift).
  2. Purges all attached photos and audio voice notes.
  3. Resets memory states and telemetry, disassociating past analytics tracking from future activity.

Cloud Backups can be removed via Settings → Clear Cloud Backups, or by revoking access through your Google Account Security or Apple ID settings.
